## Brand & Style

This design system blends collegiate authority with modern institutional intelligence. Designed for ambitious students, prospective candidates, distinguished faculty, and recruiters, the aesthetic projects scholarship, technological rigor, and institutional heritage.

The visual direction merges **Minimalist Precision** with **Elevated Collegiate Editorial**:
- **Structured Architectural Grids:** Inspired by classic university brick facades and academic folios, balanced with spacious modern margins.
- **Editorial Authority:** Scholarly headlines anchor the layout with historical weight, contrasted by crisp, human-centric sans-serif body typography.
- **Collegiate Trim:** Rich deep crimson and warm regal gold accents provide focal points against architectural charcoal surfaces and warm parchment-leaning off-whites.
- **Academic Warmth:** Avoiding sterile enterprise tech tropes, the interface uses measured warmth, fine-line detailing, and confident, deliberate interactive surfaces.

## Colors

The color hierarchy reinforces institutional heritage while delivering clear hierarchy for complex informational tasks:

- **Primary (`#1A1A24` - Slate Charcoal Navy):** Anchors headers, primary interactive states, structural framing, high-emphasis text, and prestigious dark panel surfaces.
- **Secondary (`#7B1113` - Collegiate Crimson):** Emphasizes vital institutional actions, selected tabs, badges, institutional alerts, and high-impact navigational markers.
- **Tertiary (`#D4AF37` - Regal Gold):** Used for honors, high-tier placement statistics, active system badges, notification indicators, and micro-accents.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F8FAFC` - Off-White Parchment):** The foundation for expansive, glare-free reading during prolonged research sessions and conversational engagement.

### Tonal Accents & Surface Allocation
- **Surface Elevation 0:** `#F8FAFC` (Canvas background)
- **Surface Elevation 1:** `#FFFFFF` (Card surfaces, chat message containers, input fields)
- **Surface Elevation 2:** `#F1F5F9` (Subtle container wells, conversational assistant bubbles)
- **Structural Outlines:** `#E2E8F0` with subtle charcoal tinting for crisp division without visual weight.
- **Text Tiers:** Primary `#1A1A24`, Secondary `#475569`, Muted `#94A3B8`.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the literary gravitas of **Newsreader** with the clean, metric-driven legibility of **Manrope**.

- **Editorial Headlines (Newsreader):** Retains academic dignity. Used exclusively for primary page headers, conversational greeting hero blocks, modal titles, and institutional section anchors. Use medium-to-semibold weights with tight tracking.
- **Interface & Conversational Body (Manrope):** Ensures rapid reading comprehension across density-heavy academic data such as syllabus lists, placement matrices, and chat streams.
- **Labels & Microcopy (Manrope):** Capitalized or tracking-boosted small labels provide structured clarity for meta tags, department badges, and quick-action prompts.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout utilizes an intentional 12-column architectural grid on desktop devices, scaling dynamically down to an 8-column layout on tablets and a 4-column layout on mobile viewports.

### Rhythm & Proportions
- **Canvas Margins:** Fixed `2rem` (32px) margins frame the academic workspace, creating an editorial border around the dashboard. Mobile reduces to `1rem` (16px).
- **Workspace Division:** The assistant maintains a split-pane layout on desktop: an authoritative 320px persistent quick-access drawer (Admissions, Syllabus, Placements, Faculty), balanced alongside a fluid main conversation and context console.
- **Rhythmic Scale:** Spacing follows an 8pt base grid for structural alignments and 4pt increments for micro-alignments, ensuring compact data readability in statistical placement tables and comfortable air in chat flows.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses **tonal layer stacking** combined with **tailored academic ambient shadows**, avoiding exaggerated floating planes in favor of grounded physical stationery.

- **Baseline Level 0 (Parchment Foundation):** `#F8FAFC`, non-elevated flat canvas.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Foundation):** Pure white `#FFFFFF` layered with a whisper border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`) and an ambient tinted shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(26, 26, 36, 0.04), 0 1px 2px rgba(26, 26, 36, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Dialogs, Contextual Popovers & Quick Actions):** Elevated panels use `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(26, 26, 36, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(26, 26, 36, 0.04)` with a crisp charcoal top-rim border (`1px solid #CBD5E1`).
- **Level 3 (Modals & Full Campus Overlays):** Deep ambient spread `0 20px 32px -8px rgba(26, 26, 36, 0.16)` with `#1A1A24` at 40% opacity backdrop wash.
- **Conversational Depth:** Assistant responses sit flat on Level 1 card surfaces or slight tint `#F1F5F9`, while user prompts adopt the deep `#1A1A24` charcoal ground with high-contrast white text.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a **tailored soft architecture** (`roundedness: 1`). This provides geometric stability with humanized corners:

- **Base Corner Radius (`0.25rem` / 4px):** Applied to form elements, checkboxes, table cells, and status indicators.
- **Container Radius (`rounded-lg: 0.5rem` / 8px):** Applied to conversational response panels, dashboard tiles, quick-action tiles, and drawer navigation.
- **Panel Radius (`rounded-xl: 0.75rem` / 12px):** Applied to major surface containers, modals, and institutional alert banners.
- **Conversational Bubbles Exception:** User messages adopt an asymmetric profile (`8px 8px 2px 8px`) and assistant responses use (`8px 8px 8px 2px`) to subtly establish dialogue orientation while respecting the restrained radius scale.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Institutional Button:** Solid `#1A1A24` charcoal background, white Manrope text, `0.25rem` radius, `0.5rem 1rem` padding. On hover, shifts to crimson `#7B1113` with smooth 150ms transition.
- **Secondary Action Button:** Transparent surface with `1px solid #1A1A24` border and dark charcoal text. Hover brings `#F1F5F9` background.
- **Tertiary / Honor Button:** Accentuated with `#D4AF37` gold outline, deep charcoal text, and gold-tinted hover fill.

### Quick Action Chips (Admissions, Placements, Syllabus, Navigation)
- Compact, pill-adjacent (`0.25rem` radius), light background (`#FFFFFF`), `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
- Includes a leading micro-icon (e.g., compass, cap, scroll).
- Hover state: Border shifts to `#7B1113` (Crimson) with subtle gold text hint.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Background: `#FFFFFF`.
- Inactive State: `1px solid #CBD5E1`.
- Focused State: `1px solid #1A1A24` accompanied by a `2px` focus halo in warm gold tone (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.25)`).
- Typography: 14px Manrope with deep `#1A1A24` input color and `#94A3B8` placeholder.

### Conversational Feed & Chat Bubbles
- **User Message:** `#1A1A24` background, `#FFFFFF` text, aligned right, crisp 8px rounding with tailored bottom-right anchoring corner.
- **Assistant Message:** `#FFFFFF` background, `1px solid #E2E8F0` border, `#1A1A24` text with Newsreader accents for citations or summary leads. Includes an institutional monogram crest avatar.
- **Source & Citation Badges:** Subtle `11px` capsules pinned below responses linking directly to official syllabus PDFs or placement catalogs.

### Cards & Institutional Data Tiles
- Constructed on clean `#FFFFFF` with minimal hairline border `#E2E8F0`.
- Card headers utilize Newsreader semibold typography with an optional crimson (`#7B1113`) vertical edge accent (3px width) on the left margin for urgent notices or admissions deadlines.
- Stat indicators (such as highest placement packages or cut-off scores) present figures in bold Manrope paired with gold `#D4AF37` micro-tags.
